Privacy Policy

How FMASTER handles your data — short, honest, and on-device.

01

On-device only

All calculations run locally. Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly export it.

02

Zero analytics

No tracking SDKs, no ad IDs, no third-party telemetry. The app never phones home.

03

Yours to wipe

Clear all stored data with a single tap from Settings → Data → Clear history.

Overview

FMASTER respects your privacy. This page summarises the principles that govern how the app handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you put into the calculator stays on the device that produced it.

This policy applies only to the FMASTER iOS application. It does not extend to any third-party websites you may reach through links in our in-app legal pages or marketing material.

Local-only Processing

All calculations are performed locally on this device. The app does not transmit formula inputs, intermediate values, or results to any external server. Even when you don't have an internet connection, FMASTER works fully — the engine is bundled with the binary.

History & Preferences

Two categories of data are stored only in local app storage on your device:

  • Calculation history — formulas, inputs, results, and timestamps for any computation you choose to save.
  • User preferences — your haptic feedback toggle (Settings → Preferences) and decimal place setting (Settings → Preferences).

Both can be cleared at any time. Open the app, navigate to Settings → Data → Clear history, and confirm. Removal is immediate and irreversible.

What We Don't Collect
  • Personal identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number.
  • Location data, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, MAC, IMEI, serial numbers).
  • Anonymous usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioural tracking.
  • Any information shared with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
Third-party SDKs

FMASTER does not bundle any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDKs. The app's compiled binary contains only the code and content required to perform the calculations described above.

Children's Privacy

FMASTER is a general-audience engineering reference. It does not knowingly collect any data from any user, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the app's Settings → Privacy section. Continued use of FMASTER after an update constitutes acceptance of the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or to report a concern, contact the developer at the email below. We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe.
