Frequently asked questions
// knowledge base
Where is my calculation history stored? +

Every saved calculation lives only on your device — in the app's local storage. Nothing is uploaded. You can inspect, export or wipe history from Settings → Data.

How do I export my calculations? +

Open Settings → Data → Export history. FMASTER creates a JSON file of all saved entries and opens the iOS share sheet so you can save to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or send it by mail.

Can I work entirely offline? +

Yes. The calculator engine and the complete library of dimensionless numbers are bundled with the binary. Airplane mode is a fully supported runtime.

How do I delete all my data? +

Open the app, go to Settings → Data → Clear history, and confirm. The action is irreversible but immediate. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes everything.

I think a number is wrong. Where do I report it? +

Use the contact form below or email Butraminamhegqulad@gmail.com with the formula name and inputs you used. We cross-check reported cases against source literature before pushing a fix.

Does the app use my data for anything? +

No. FMASTER does not transmit inputs, results, or any telemetry to any server. There are no analytics SDKs, ad networks, or third-party trackers bundled in the binary. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the number of decimals shown? +

Open Settings → Preferences → Decimal places and use the stepper to choose between 1 and 8 decimals. The setting is preserved across sessions.

Why don't haptics fire when I toggle the switch off? +

That's the intended behaviour. Disabling haptics in Settings → Preferences silences vibration feedback across the calculator, gradient buttons and insight donut. The setting takes effect immediately.
